## 1. How every price is set — one rule for every county

**The benchmark.** Detailed Drivers publishes all-in prices (20% gratuity, tolls and tax included) for its Cadillac Escalade ESV class across Manhattan, Long Island, Westchester, Connecticut and New Jersey, with the road miles of each route. Their 24 published journeys follow one line, each within ±5%: **$150 + $6.94 per road mile** (19 of them are journeys we serve and are compared one by one in section 2). On the Manhattan airport routes that line sits with NYC Lux Ride's Escalade ESV and below Gotham Ride, so it is the market's Escalade price, measured the same way in every region.

**Our regular price follows the same line**, so every county is priced exactly as the market prices an Escalade there:

1. **Regular price = $150 + $6.94 × road miles** of the client's journey, rounded to the nearest $5. All-in: tolls, congestion charges and gratuity included. The same price in either direction.
2. **Long journeys:** if lower, the round trip at the hourly rate ($150/h, also the benchmark's hourly rate) + 30 minutes' turnaround + tolls — the market prices distance work by time. Marked † below.
3. **Never below cost:** committed vehicle time × $65/h + running cost $0.60/mile + tolls + fixed costs + card fees. Marked ‡ below (airport-to-suburb journeys where the car's empty drives make the trip longer than its miles).
4. **Road miles:** the benchmark's published distance where it has one (marked *); otherwise estimated from the map — straight line × a road factor fitted on the benchmark's 24 published routes (city trips wind more than highway runs). Jersey Shore counties are measured through the Parkway/Turnpike corridor at Woodbridge, the only way north around Raritan Bay. Each county is priced from one point where most trips start; tell me a real distance and it replaces the estimate.
5. **Hourly:** $150/hour all-in, 3-hour minimum (benchmark: $150/hour).
6. **Introductory rate:** 10% off every regular price (rounded down to $5, so never less than 10%) until January 31, 2027. Then the regular price applies and must be kept for at least 30 days (NYC rule 6 RCNY § 5-95). Active.

**Fit to the benchmark:** across its 19 published journeys our regular price is within -6% to +5% (median +0%). Journeys committing the vehicle for more than 8 hours are quoted individually.

## 7. Keeping it current

- Re-check the benchmark and competitors each quarter; update `data.BENCHMARKS`, run `python3 tools/rate_model.py --write`. The tests fail if any published benchmark journey drifts more than 7% from our price.
- Raise prices once a year in January, by a small step tied to real costs (tolls, insurance, fuel), with 30 days' notice. Never change a booked trip.
- No surge pricing. Busy days are handled by limited time slots, not price.
