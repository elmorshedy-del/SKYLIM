    Questions

    Before you reserve.

    Anything else? reservations@celesteluxurylimo.com

    Where do you operate?

    Across New Jersey, New York City, Westchester, Long Island, Fairfield County and Philadelphia — including Newark, JFK, LaGuardia, Teterboro, Morristown and Westchester airports. For anything further, ask; we arrange long-distance journeys regularly.

    How is the price calculated?

    Point-to-point journeys are priced on the time and distance involved, including tolls, congestion charges and gratuity. Hourly service is a single all-inclusive hourly rate with a three-hour minimum. The price you confirm is the price you pay.

    How quickly is my reservation confirmed?

    Usually the moment payment clears. Before a time is offered, our system checks the calendar — the drive to you, your journey and the drive back — so a confirmed reservation is one we can keep. Very short notice, unusual luggage or routes we arrange personally are confirmed by us first, typically within the hour.

    Can I keep the car for the day?

    Yes. Book by the hour and your chauffeur stays with you between meetings — plans can change on the day. There is a three-hour minimum.

    Is this a limousine or a black car service?

    A private chauffeur service — what many people call a black car service. Every journey is in a Cadillac Escalade rather than a stretch limousine, reserved in advance and driven by a professional chauffeur, with the price agreed before you travel.

    Do you serve private aviation?

    Yes — Teterboro, Morristown and Westchester. Give us your tail number or FBO and we will meet you there.

    What vehicle will arrive?

    A Cadillac Escalade with second-row captain's chairs, a panoramic sunroof and onboard Wi-Fi. Before pickup, your trip page shows your chauffeur's name, the vehicle and its plate.

    Can I see where my car is?

    Yes. When your chauffeur sets off, your private trip page shows the vehicle's actual position and when it was last reported. If the signal drops, the page says so rather than guessing. Location is shown only during your journey and is deleted afterwards.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Complimentary cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Within 24 hours, refunds are reviewed individually, because the time was reserved for you.

    How much luggage fits?

    Up to six guests. With every seat in use there is room for 2 large checked bags and 3 carry-ons. With four guests, part of the third row folds for 4 large bags and 4 carry-ons; with up to three guests, the third row folds for 6 large bags and 4 carry-ons. The booking form shows you instantly whether your luggage fits.

    Is a child seat available?

    Yes, on request at no charge. Tell us the child's age and weight when you reserve and we will confirm the fit.

    Is my information private?

    Your details are used only to provide your journey. Payment is handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card number. Trip links are private and expire.
