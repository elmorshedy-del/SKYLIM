We want booking and riding with us to work for everyone.

The website

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA: every form field is labelled, the site works with a keyboard and screen readers, text has strong contrast, and motion is reduced if your device asks for it.

The ride

Tell us in your booking notes about anything that would help — extra time at pickup, help with bags, a folding mobility aid, or a service animal (always welcome). The vehicle is not wheelchair-accessible; if you need a ramp-equipped vehicle we will tell you honestly rather than take the booking.

Tell us what to fix

If anything on the site or in the car gets in your way, email us and we will reply personally.
