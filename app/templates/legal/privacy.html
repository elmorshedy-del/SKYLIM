What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, email, mobile number, pickup and drop-off addresses, trip time, party size, flight number if you give one, notes and cabin preferences (including child-seat details if you ask for one).
  • Payment: handled entirely by Stripe. We receive confirmation that you paid and the amount; we never see or store your card number.
  • Location during your trip: the chauffeur's phone or the car reports its position while your trip is under way. We do not collect your location.
  • Website usage: anonymous counts of steps in the booking form, without personal details.

How we use it

Only to run your trip, contact you about it, keep required business records and improve the service. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not send marketing unless you ask.

Location and trip links

Your trip page is private to anyone holding its link. The car's position is shown only while your trip is in progress and is deleted when the trip ends. A share link you create shows only the car's position and status, and stops working when the trip ends.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the business — Stripe (payments), our email provider, and our hosting and database providers — under their own privacy and security terms. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting it

Booking and payment records are kept as long as tax and business rules require. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing us.
